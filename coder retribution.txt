3. CURRENT FOCUS: localllm\  (build + train your own LLM, from scratch)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Location: Projects\srlm-forge\localllm\   Only dependency: torch.
  model.py       from-scratch GPT (decoder-only Transformer, training-ready)
  data.py        dependency-free CHAR tokenizer built from your corpus + batching
  train.py       train from RANDOM init; cosine LR, bf16, checkpoints, samples
  generate.py    sample from a model you trained
  make_corpus.py builds a demo corpus.txt from this project's own source+solutions
  README.md      the pitch + quickstart + honest scale limits

RUN IT (from the localllm dir, with the venv python):
  cd C:\Users\t\Projects\srlm-forge\localllm
  ..\.venv-train\Scripts\python.exe make_corpus.py          # or bring your own .txt
  ..\.venv-train\Scripts\python.exe train.py --data corpus.txt --steps 2000 --out mymodel
  ..\.venv-train\Scripts\python.exe generate.py --out mymodel --prompt "def solve("
Scale via --n-layer --n-head --n-embd --block-size --batch-size; train longer w/ --steps.

VERIFIED RESULT (2026-07-25): trained a 3.18M-param char GPT from random init on a
549 KB corpus -> val loss 4.41 -> 0.17 in ~17 s on the 4080; generates Python-shaped
code. Demo model saved at localllm\out_demo\ (ckpt.pt + tokenizer.json; git-ignored).

HONEST SCALE CAVEAT (tell the user, don't oversell): single-GPU from-scratch = small
models (a few M to a few hundred M params) that learn a corpus's structure/style.
Real from-scratch LLM, fully owned/local — but NOT a GPT-4 competitor (those need
thousands of GPUs + internet-scale data).
