### The refuted gap, in one line

**The Sketch Law** was killed by Nikolich et al., *Weight-Space Geometry of Offline Reasoning Training* (arXiv 2606.23740) §3.4 — which the prior-work refuter reports as already publishing cross-seed near-orthogonality of same-task LoRA deltas for six losses including DPO, its attribution to the unseeded random `lora_A`, the shared-left/chance-right singular-direction asymmetry (u1 ≈ 0.99 vs v1 ≈ 0.07), a no-barrier interpolation of the deltas themselves, and the verbatim conclusion "functionally the seeds are the same solution" — reinforced by LottaLoRA (*A Little Rank Goes a Long Way*, arXiv 2604.08749), which independently states three of the gap's headline payoffs, and by the kernel/lazy-regime canon (Malladi et al., *A Kernel-Based View of Language Model Fine-Tuning*; Woodworth et al., *Kernel and Rich Regimes in Overparametrized Models*; Chizat et al., *On Lazy Training in Differentiable Programming*).

**Refuted as a mechanism, not as an experiment.** The framings that must be dropped, precisely:

- "dW is a random sketch of one data-determined gradient" — this is LoRA-One (*One-Step Full Gradient Could Suffice*, arXiv 2502.01235) with a random rather than gradient-chosen A0.
- "The function is a deterministic functional of (base, data, program) while the weights are a random family" — the kernel-regime min-norm theorem, stated for LLM LoRA in *Linearization Explains Fine-Tuning in Large Language Models* (arXiv 2602.08239).
- "A is a frozen random projection, B carries the function; left subspaces shared, right at chance" — Zhu et al., *Asymmetry in Low-Rank Adapters of Foundation Models* (arXiv 2402.16842), plus LoRA-FA and VeRA.
- "Rank at which agreement saturates estimates k_eff" — Li et al., *Measuring the Intrinsic Dimension of Objective Landscapes* (ICLR 2018), and LottaLoRA verbatim.
- "Averaging orthogonal adapters is variance reduction, not basin averaging" — Wang, Gittens & Mahoney, *Sketched Ridge Regression* and *No Free Lunch From Random Feature Ensembles* (arXiv 2412.05418), which predict the rank-32 concatenation behaves as one rank-32 sketch with no sqrt(2) correction.
- "Weight-space cosine is the wrong instrument, function space is the right one" — Ding, Denain & Steinhardt, *Grounding Representation Similarity with Statistical Testing*, and Schiffman, *Transformers converge to invariant algorithmic cores* (arXiv 2602.22600), which already shows independently trained transformers embedding cores in nearly orthogonal subspaces while recovering identical spectra.
- "Adapter = seed + B" — LottaLoRA and *Kilobyte Models* (arXiv 2608.00860).

**What remains genuinely new** (both refuters agree on the same residue):

1. The **dose-response location in behavior space**. Theory says a lazy-to-rich boundary exists; no paper locates it for preference optimization, says what marks it, or shows it in per-item behavior. Zero papers were found measuring cross-seed item-level agreement as a function of training dose for DPO. The non-derivable claim is that the marker is **reward-margin saturation** (retrain logs: margins ~0.78, train_loss 0.582/0.583/0.584; `training_poscontrol.log`: margins 10-11, accuracy 1.0 from ~epoch 1.7, train_loss 0.121) rather than step size, and **not** A-displacement, which Probe 2 rules out (96% of A entries inside the kaiming box even at 40x lr and 3 epochs).
2. The **empirical contradiction of the standing default**. Summers & Dinneen (*Nondeterminism and Instability in Neural Network Optimization*) and Bui et al. (*Assessing the Macro and Micro Effects of Random Seeds*, arXiv 2503.07329, which finds *low* per-instance consistency) are the field's expectation. Reliability-corrected per-task agreement at the instrument ceiling in a generative, execution-verified setting contradicts it. "Known in principle, believed false in practice" is publishable; "known in principle" is not.
3. The **seed-variance floor as a correction to live literature**: measured cross-run dW cosine 0.0042 +/- 0.0044 against the analytic 1/sqrt(r*fan_in) = 0.0039, which is the null that Nikolich et al.'s "DPO sits in a near-orthogonal subspace" and *Naive LoRA Summation* (arXiv 2508.11985) lack. One figure, not a paper.
4. The **TRL seeding-order defect** and a released-adapter audit — engineering meta-science with no owner in any adjacent field, and self-undercutting as theory (if the function is seed-invariant, an unseeded init is nearly harmless).

## 2. Rank 1 — Optimizer-state quantization breaks Adam's trust region at a model-determined address

**Central claim.** Blockwise 8-bit optimizer-state quantization as deployed in bitsandbytes (blocksize 256, both moments stored `uint8` with a per-block absmax) ceases to implement Adam inside any quantization block that contains an outlier gradient coordinate, and in Llama-family models the massive-activation channel guarantees such a coordinate at a fixed, model-determined address — so the violation is deterministic, reproducible, locatable before training, and present in every 8-bit-Adam QLoRA run on such a model, while the training record says `optim = "adamw_bnb_8bit"`. Because Adam's own per-coordinate displacement bound (Kingma & Ba 2015, Sec. 2.1: |Delta_t| <= alpha*(1-beta1)/sqrt(1-beta2), which is 3.16*alpha at the defaults, described there as a trust region) holds regardless of seed, data order or kernel nondeterminism, the bound doubles as a replica-free, recomputation-free, seed-free post-hoc detector — and lazy-A LoRA keeps benign runs so far inside it that the separation is usable.

**Mechanism.** dL/dA[:, j] scales with the j-th input activation. Probe 4 measured, on the dequantized `layers.1.mlp.down_proj` of the cached base with one forward pass on a code prompt, max|x| = 386.5 at intermediate channel 2427 against 46.3 for the next channel (198) and a median of 0.013 across 14,336 channels — a factor of 30,000. That channel's second-moment state sets the absmax of its 256-element block; its 255 neighbours' v underflow the 8-bit dynamic format; since the step scales as 1/sqrt(v), their effective steps explode. The signature matches exactly: of all 224 `lora_A` tensors in `dpo_adapter_poscontrol`, only `layers.1.mlp.down_proj.lora_A` has any element beyond init bound + 3.16*Sum(lr) = 0.0547 — 169 of them, all inside columns [2304, 2558] (the 256-aligned block 9), across 93 distinct columns and all 16 rank rows, max |a| = 0.175 (21x the init bound 1/sqrt(14336) = 0.0084, 3x the worst-case Adam bound), mean per-column max|a| 0.058 inside vs 0.009 outside — and **column 2427 itself is not an outlier (0.0044)**, which is what the absmax-holder prediction requires. Only one stray element elsewhere (`layers.31.mlp.up_proj.lora_B` at 0.0557).

**Prior art that must be cited as background, not claimed.** The adjacent refuter is right that two of three pillars are relabelings. The bound is Kingma & Ba Sec. 2.1 verbatim. The mechanism — a blockwise absmax dominated by an in-block outlier, under-resolving small second moments, producing "catastrophically large deviation of the update direction" — is published in Li, Chen & Zhu, *Memory Efficient Optimizers with 4-bit States* (NeurIPS 2023, arXiv 2309.01507), including that moment outliers persist in particular columns and that a block containing an outlier gets a large scale that misrepresents its small elements; the wider v-underestimation family is Shazeer & Stern's Adafactor update clipping and Wortsman/Dettmers et al.'s StableAdamW.

**What is genuinely new**, and what no search found:

1. An empirical existence proof that the **deployed** bitsandbytes 8-bit dynamic blockwise format violates the Adam bound by up to 3x in a real QLoRA run — a direct counterexample to the accuracy-parity claim of Dettmers et al., *8-bit Optimizers via Block-wise Quantization* (arXiv 2110.02861), which Li et al. never falsified and which the only recent quantized-state analysis, Topollai & Choromanska (arXiv 2603.16731), contradicts in the opposite direction (they report staleness, i.e. updates too small).
2. The chain massive-activation channel -> LoRA-A gradient outlier -> deterministic 256-aligned block address. arXiv full-text search for "massive activations" + LoRA returns **zero** papers; Yu et al., *The Super Weight in Large Language Models* (arXiv 2411.07191) does not list Llama-3-8B and is inference-quantization only; Subramanian et al., *Super Weights in LLMs and the Failure of Selective Training* (arXiv 2607.08733) studies LoRA at super-weight coordinates with no optimizer-state content; *Attention Sinks Induce Gradient Sinks* (arXiv 2603.17771) establishes the gradient-concentration half but nothing else.
3. The bound as a post-hoc integrity invariant. Every existing detector needs something this machine does not have: a replica (Ma et al., arXiv 2502.12340; SCOUT, arXiv 2608.11034), online monitoring with an empirically calibrated threshold plus step recomputation (Altenbernd et al., arXiv 2604.00726), trace-inferred invariants (TrainCheck, arXiv 2506.14813), or structure-only certification (Shang et al., *Structured Drift Proofs*, arXiv 2604.04738; VeriLoRA).

**Minimal falsifying experiment, zero-compute first.**

- `adam_bound_scan.py` over all 448 tensors, binned by (layer, module, 256-aligned flat block), reconstructing Sum(lr) from `training_args.bin` with transformers' own `get_cosine_schedule_with_warmup` (171 steps, 18 warmup, cosine, lr 2e-4 -> Sum(lr) = 0.01730). `block_energy.py` for the share of ||dW||_F^2 per block (measured: 70.4% of that layer's, 1.8% if uniform; 0.94% of the whole adapter's).
- `quantized_adam_emulator.py`: numpy Adam with 256-block absmax-quantized second moments under the bnb dynamic map, fed gradient columns with one channel ~100x the others. This is the discriminator between v-underflow, first-moment quantization, the dynamic map's minimum representable value, and gradient-clipping interplay.
- Read the installed bitsandbytes 0.50.1 optimizer path to confirm the premises rather than infer them: `optim/optimizer.py` min_8bit_size default 4096 (lines 414, 604), fp32 fallback iff `numel < min_8bit_size` (502, 674), blocksize 256 (524, 691), both m (dynamic signed qmap) and v (udynamic unsigned) as `uint8` with per-256-block absmax, dispatch to `F.optimizer_update_8bit_blockwise` (570, 736; `functional.py:1169`). Every LoRA tensor has numel in {16384, 65536, 229376}, all >= 4096, so all 448 are 8-bit.

**Cheapest GPU step.** Four fixed-seed `--max-steps 20` runs at lr 2e-4 (~10-12 min each, ~50 min total): `adamw_bnb_8bit`, `adamw_torch`, `paged_adamw_8bit`, and `adamw_bnb_8bit` with `min_8bit_size` raised above 229,376. With a seeded A0 the exact displacement A_trained - A_init is measurable for all 224 tensors. Add a 1-step gradient hook on `layers.1.mlp.down_proj.lora_A.grad`: channel 2427's grad^2 against its block-mates' median, and the same for channel 198's block [0, 255], whose within-block dynamic range is about 70x smaller and should therefore show a weaker effect or none.

**What would refute it.** `adamw_torch` also produces elements beyond the bound (the cause is clipping, a kernel fault, or TDR — the invariant survives, the story collapses; check the logged `grad_norm`, since `max_grad_norm` is 1.0). The excess is not block-aligned on rerun (a non-reproducible event, which reopens F4). It moves to a different window under a different seed or corpus (not column-conditioned). It depends on paging rather than bit width (the `paged` and `min_8bit_size` arms separate these). Or `min_8bit_size` raised does not remove it.

**What confirmation establishes.** A deterministic, address-periodic, model-conditioned software corruption class — reproducible where hardware SDC is not — plus the first optimizer-derived integrity invariant for fine-tuning, plus a concrete recommendation: use 32-bit optimizer states for LoRA parameters, where the memory saving on ~42M parameters is about 0.3 GB, i.e. nil. It also flags that cosines, Frobenius norms, and intruder-dimension counts computed over QLoRA adapters trained with 8-bit state on a massive-activation model are partly measuring a quantization artifact — which reaches the LoRA-geometry literature, not just this paper.

**How it repositions the manuscript.** The section *An 8-Bit Optimizer Artifact Localized by a Massive-Activation Channel* moves from a curiosity late in Results to a co-headline; Limitation 3c is discharged; Failure 7's `optim` field stops being a mis-description and becomes a measured defect; and F4's TDR hypothesis acquires a mundane, demonstrable competitor for "a silent non-Adam update with no exception" — one that costs 50 minutes to test instead of requiring admin rights the machine does not have.

## 3. Rank 2 — A functional acceptance class for "the update took"

**Central claim.** For a training procedure whose legitimate outcomes are weight-orthogonal adapters implementing one function, "the update took" can only mean membership in that functional outcome class; corruption phenotypes separate in function space by fault type (omission -> a scaled-down copy of the legitimate function; commission -> an uncorrelated direction at preserved norm, indistinguishable from a norm-matched random adapter); and the original null adapter — same norm class, zero mean, split-half reliability 0.21 (session A) / 0.52 (session B), profile r = -0.10 with the retrains — is classifiable as commission/random without invoking TDR at all.

**Framing that must be dropped.** "No functional or behavioral oracle exists for nondeterministic computation" is false: Baker et al., *A new ensemble-based consistency test for the Community Earth System Model* (pyCECT, GMD 8:2829, 2015) accepts a non-bitwise-reproducible run iff it is statistically indistinguishable from an ensemble of trusted runs, and is used to catch software and hardware errors; HECT (arXiv 2010.04051) continues the line. The honest claim is that no such oracle exists **for ML training runs**, and the contribution is porting ensemble-consistency acceptance into function space. Both phenotypes are also corollaries, not discoveries: omission -> scaled-down follows from lazy-regime linearity (Chizat et al., arXiv 1812.07956) plus sparsified-gradient convergence (Alistarh et al., arXiv 1809.10505); commission -> uncorrelated at preserved norm is concentration of measure, and FLTrust (arXiv 2012.13995) already accepts updates by cosine to a trusted reference with norm normalization, while *A Little Is Enough* (arXiv 1902.06156) warns that norm-bounded corruptions can evade direction-based detection.

**What is genuinely new**: (a) a replica-free, generation-free, post-hoc acceptance test for a single finished adapter calibrated on healthy-retrain dispersion; (b) the norm-matched random-LoRA null arm for an improvement claim — no arXiv precedent, since Ilharco et al.'s random-vector control (*Editing Models with Task Arithmetic*) was for negation on ViT/GPT-2 and Li et al.'s filter-normalized random directions (*Visualizing the Loss Landscape*) were for landscape geometry; (c) empirical separability of omission vs commission at LoRA-DPO scale under a 31-task instrument of reliability 0.2-0.9 — arXiv returns zero hits for "fault injection" + LoRA; (d) Ding-Feller-Miratrix randomization inference, Crump et al.'s nonparametric CATE tests, and PERMANOVA applied to LLM benchmark replicate matrices, together with the demonstration that effect-variation criteria alone cannot separate a trained-null adapter from norm-matched noise while reliability and correlation can; (e) the classification of the original adapter against the calibrated class.

**Minimal falsifying experiment.** Zero-compute first: `profile_stats.py` over the 334 rows of `data/ruler_noise.jsonl` — every row carries the 31-task `sampled=[0/1 x5]` lists, so DFM, PERMANOVA, replicate-bootstrap distances to the rep1/2/3 centroid, and pass@k for k <= 5 are all computable today. Cheapest GPU: train one healthy adapter at the paper recipe (25 min — note this is a projection, never yet measured on this box), export (4 min), build the norm-matched random arm on that adapter's own A0 plus a 0.5x variant, then a 15-round 3-arm interleaved chain (~115 min).

**Refuted if** the original adapter sits inside the healthy class once instrument noise is accounted for; or the random adapter's profile is reliable and correlates with the healthy profile above 0.5, which places the pipeline in the *Neural Thickets* regime (arXiv 2603.12228: 8% to 64% of random sigma = 0.005 perturbations match or beat the base across 0.5B-32B, Llama-3.1-8B included); or healthy-vs-healthy fingerprint cosines overlap corrupted-vs-healthy.

**Establishes** integrity-as-effect as distinct from integrity-as-structure, and settles the manuscript's fourth reading by measurement rather than narrative. **Repositions** the Discussion: the TDR demotion stops being a hedge and becomes a classification with a named test. Two hard constraints: scipy is not installed in `.venv-train` (numpy 2.5.2 is), so DFM and PERMANOVA must be pure-numpy permutation implementations, cribbing the local Welch/incomplete-beta code in `poscontrol/compare_replication.py`; and only 5 of the original 40 `llama3-forged-null` rows are on this machine, so the classification is behavioral against 4090 same-session nulls only.

## 4. Rank 3 — Construct-valid gain as invariance under a verifier-environment swap

**Central claim.** Construct-valid gain is the component of a measured benchmark gain that survives re-scoring **fixed weights and retained generations** in a perturbed verifier environment, and the governing parameter for verifier error is its learnability, not its false-positive rate.

**Framings that must be dropped.** "Compliance before competence via minority-contrast amplification" is dead twice over. As a phenomenon class it is a relabeling: Shah et al., *The Pitfalls of Simplicity Bias*; Zhao et al., *Men Also Like Shopping* (bias amplification is a named, quantified metric); Pezeshki et al., *Gradient Starvation*; and for DPO specifically Park et al., *Disentangling Length from Quality in Direct Preference Optimization*, with Dubois et al.'s length-controlled AlpacaEval as the exact counterfactual-decomposition precedent. And empirically it is dead on the data: the census run read-only during the feasibility audit returns **f_typing = 0 of 918** — zero typing-only rejected pairs, zero chosen halves containing `from typing import`, zero annotations anywhere in prompts, chosen or rejected (taxonomy: AssertionError 773, TypeError 56, IndexError 41, NameError 17 all lru_cache/helper-scope, SyntaxError 8, timeout 6, other 17). The manuscript already reports this. So the typing-ablated, import-neutralized, typing-only and 25%-dose corpora are all degenerate, and the gap's own preregistered refuting branch fires immediately. Separately, "train on broken vs hardened signal at fixed compute" has been executed: *When the Reward Suite Is Leaky* (arXiv 2607.11022), preregistered, natural false positives, MBPP vs MBPP+ under GRPO — and it found **selection without amplification**.

**What survives, and it is stranger than the original framing.** How does DPO on a corpus with **zero** typing content teach the policy to stop tripping the verifier's typing NameError on annotated benchmark prompts, dropping typing failures from ~11 per 155 generations (every null arm and the original adapter: 10.20-14.67) to 2.05-2.60 in the retrains and 0.11 in the positive control? Two mechanisms — emit-the-import versus stop-annotating — are currently indistinguishable because no completions were retained. And no paper documents interpreter-version-dependent ground truth as a measured training-signal phenomenon: an arXiv query for "Python version" + "code generation" + "evaluation" returned totalResults = 0, and 37 hits for interpreter-version phrasings contained nothing on version-dependent verdict flips.

**Minimal falsifying experiment.** Zero-compute first: Welch tests on per-row `aggregate_if_typing_imported` for every arm against its same-session null, typing-fail counts with replicate-bootstrap CIs, and a task-feature regression on annotated-signature presence. Cheapest step, and it is CPU-only: patch `eval.py`'s evaluate loop (~lines 259-271, which currently discards completions after `forge.verify`) to retain completions and store dual verdicts per candidate in one row — the drift guard means both verdicts must be stored together rather than obtained by re-running under a second pin — then re-score with a `from typing import *` prelude injected. That needs no new interpreter. The stronger arm installs Python 3.14 per-user (winget, or python.org with `InstallAllUsers=0`, or `uv python install`), about 10 minutes and no admin; under 3.14 the null's base rate also moves, so only adapter-minus-null within each verifier is interpretable and the [0.2, 0.8] screen was calibrated under the old pin.

**Refuted if** the re-executed gain matches the raw figure, meaning the counter is not identifying what it claims.

**Establishes** a reporting standard: the instrument-conditional share of any reported gain, for every code-DPO/RLVR paper whose benchmark prompts carry annotations (AceCoder, PLUM, CodeDPO, Sol-Ver). **Repositions** Limitation 3a from a bound into a measurement.

## 7. What to stop doing

**Stop claiming the orthogonality result until you have read Nikolich et al. (arXiv 2606.23740) Sec. 3.4 yourself.** Your own dossier contradicts itself about it: the literature digest says their cross-objective cosines "have no seed-variance floor", while the prior-work refuter quotes Sec. 3.4 as containing exactly that floor — two seeds, three learning rates, six losses including DPO, the unseeded-A attribution, the u1/v1 asymmetry, and a no-barrier delta interpolation. The entire positioning of *The Adapter Under Test Was One Draw* and *Three Orthogonal Adapters, One Function* turns on which is true. Fetch the paper first; everything else in that section is downstream of it.

**Stop planning the rank sweep.** *Rethinking the Rank Threshold for LoRA Fine-Tuning* (arXiv 2605.03724) reduces the NTK rank prescription to r = 1 for binary losses; DPO is a binary Bradley-Terry loss. The 5.3 GPU-h session is likelier than not to return the outcome the plan itself preregistered as refuting, for reasons already in print.

**Stop planning the frozen-A arm and the left/right subspace test as findings.** Zhu et al. (arXiv 2402.16842) already show random frozen A matches trained A with an information-theoretic bound that improves; LoRA-FA and VeRA agree. Run frozen-A only as a control inside another experiment.

**Stop planning typing-ablated, import-neutralized, typing-only and 25%-dose corpora.** f_typing = 0/918. Those arms have nothing to ablate. If you want a dose axis, it must be a synthetic-injection study, which is a different (and weaker) claim.

**Stop describing the Adam displacement bound as a new invariant.** It is Kingma & Ba Sec. 2.1's trust region. The new part is that lazy-A LoRA keeps healthy runs far enough inside it to give usable separation.

**Stop claiming no functional oracle exists for nondeterministic computation.** CESM-ECT / pyCECT has been doing exactly that in climate modeling since 2015.

**Stop computing headline statistics ad hoc.** Three different values of the same profile correlation are in circulation because `profile_stats.py` does not exist. One implementation, committed, then recompute.

**Stop reporting anything that mixes machines.** Three machines with three verifier states (4080 py3.11.9 without file hashes; 4090 py3.11.9 with the 4-file fingerprint; 5080 py3.12.10). The raw per-task rate correlation between `null-rep` and `poscontrol-null` is only 0.45. Every delta goes against its same-session null, always.

**Stop treating `aggregate_if_typing_imported` as the decomposition.** It credits a failure as a pass without re-executing it. Build the retention flag, then re-execute.

**Stop starting a GPU session before the push works.** You have already lost the weight-space half of the three retrains this way — `poscontrol/adapter_replication{,2,3}` are gitignored and absent, and their Modelfiles point at `C:\Users\Kingo\...`.

**Stop citing the trace as an instrument in the present tense.** `data/trace.jsonl` does not exist; `traces.py` defines the schema and `forge.py` writes it, but no sampling run has populated it. Either populate it in a session or write about it in the future tense.

**Stop reporting the +10.3 pp TdrDelay run.** It is one comparison, in prose, with no replicate-level statistics, and it is currently carrying the weight of the article's leading causal hypothesis.

### Appendix F, entry by entry

**F1 — Where the seed-invariant regime ends. KEEP, DEMOTE from first experiment, REWRITE.** It is the best-surviving fragment of the refuted Sketch Law, and both novelty refuters independently agree that no paper measures cross-seed item-level agreement as a function of training dose for DPO. ### What the Abstract should now claim that it does not
