**Not safe to circulate as-is.** The empirical core is unusually solid — 107 independently re-derived claims matched, including every cell of Table 2, every cell of Table E1, every correlation and disattenuated *r* in Table 2b, the entire corpus-concentration table, the Welch and TOST arithmetic in Appendix A, the LoRA-init and *r*/*d*ᵢₙ derivations, and the block-alignment result at the heart of the optimizer-artifact section.
